# WTC Accra Investment Exchange roadmap

## Required before public launch

1. Replace the typographic brand lockup with the official approved WTC Accra licensed member logo asset. Keep at least 35 px clear space and do not place it over photography.
2. Connect authentication and role based access for investors, deal owners, reviewers and administrators.
3. Add KYC, AML, beneficial ownership and investor accreditation workflows with manual WTC Accra review gates.
4. Connect object storage for pitch decks, financial models and supporting documents. Add NDA acceptance, access logs and expiring links.
5. Define deal review criteria, publishing approval, conflict disclosure, risk warnings and complaint escalation.
6. Replace demonstration opportunities and metrics with approved live data. Never imply that listing means WTC Accra endorsement or guaranteed returns.
7. Add Privacy Policy, Terms, Cookie Notice, Risk Disclosure and Data Protection contacts suitable for Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012.
8. Add email notifications for submission, review, investor interest and meeting requests.
9. Add analytics for deal views, qualified leads, meetings, diligence progression and closed capital, without publishing confidential figures.
10. Complete security review, backups, restore testing, rate limiting, malware scanning and audit log retention.

## Product ideas

- Ghana opportunity index with sectors, regions, ticket sizes and investment stages.
- Curated investment rooms for trade missions, business forums and presidential or bilateral visits.
- Investor mandate profiles that privately match sector, geography, stage and cheque size.
- WTC Accra verified badge with a public explanation of exactly what was checked.
- Deal readiness score and founder checklist before submission.
- Book a confidential introduction with the WTC Accra Trade and Investment team.
- Diaspora investor pathway with Ghana focused guides and vetted professional partners.
- Post meeting pipeline with documented actions, owners and deadlines.
- Quarterly opportunity brief and opt in investor watchlists.
